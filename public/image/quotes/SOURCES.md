# Portrait sources

- Albert Einstein, Orren Jack Turner (1947): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Albert_Einstein_Head.jpg
- Abraham Lincoln, published 1914: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Abraham_Lincoln_(head).png

The Einstein and Lincoln sayings are labelled as attributed, not verified verbatim quotations. The Pareto text is a paraphrase of the principle.
